We’re a group of five students who brought our interests in anime and web design together to create ANIMORA.

ABOUT OUR GROUP

ANIMORA is a fan-inspired website made to share the stories, characters, and worlds we enjoy. Each member contributes to building and improving the site, from researching anime information and preparing page content to styling layouts and checking that everything works. This project gives us a chance to practice teamwork, creativity, and our web development skills.
